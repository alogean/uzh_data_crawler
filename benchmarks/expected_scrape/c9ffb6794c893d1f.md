# Forms and Regulations

Rules and Regulations governing the PhD in Finance are listed as "Doctorate in Business and Economics (PVO13)" on [faculty's website](https://www.oec.uzh.ch/en/studies/regulations.html).

SFI rules and reimbursement guidelines can be found on [our Intranet](https://www.df.uzh.ch/de/intranet/phd/SFI-rules-and-regulations.html).

Expense Reimbursements should be done via [Perk](https://www.fi.uzh.ch/en/fb/ap/expense.html) (AFTER prior approval by the PhD Program Coordinator).
