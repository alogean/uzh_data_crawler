# MA CFA Institute Research Challenge Training and Preparation

Seminar with Industry-Partner
Please note: This seminar will be held in English

This course is a prerequisite for the **CFA Institute Research Challenge** and equips students with the knowledge and skills needed to be fully prepared for the high-profile, demanding competition in Switzerland, Europe, and worldwide starting in September.

**[CFA Institute Research Challenge](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/events/research-challenge)**

### **Topics**

- Understanding financial reports, in isolation and in relation to each other
- Using Bloomberg to find and to analyze financial data
- Valuing businesses
- Bridging the gap from enterprise value to equity value
- Using valuation multiples
- Writing a professional equity report
- Getting meaningful numbers: willingness to pay and relative cost analysis

### **Assessment**

Students value a mid-sized, industrial Swiss company using the analytical framework developed throughout the course. They write a report and give a presentation which together determines the course grade.

Although you will need to use a spreadsheet, your report should be written in such way that the reader does not have to go through the spreadsheet to understand what you have done.

### **Textbooks**

There are no textbooks for the course.
Besides Corporate Finance: Grundlagen von Finanzierung und Investition, by R. Volkart and A. Wagner and Principles of Corporate Finance, by R. Brealey, S. Myers and F. Allen, books that may prove useful are Business Analysis and Valuation, by K. Palepu, V. Bernard, P. Healy and E. Peek, Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation, by S. Penman, Narrative and Numbers, by A. Damodaran and Pitch the Perfect Investment, by P. Sonkin and P. Johnson.

Some notes will be posted on OLAT, as well as a link to cases studies to be purchased from Harvard Business Impact.
