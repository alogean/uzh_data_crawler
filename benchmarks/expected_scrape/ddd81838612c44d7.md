# English Department Staff

![](/dam/jcr:4a6e9f40-e133-41e2-822c-91b8d0ace1fc/subsection_3.jpg)

The University of Zurich offers its employees several opportunities to spend some time abroad or to invite staff members from partner universities to spend a few days or weeks at the UZH.

For further information and guidelines on how to apply for each of the options, please have a look at the websites listed below. In case of further questions, please contact the [stay abroad coordinator](/en/studies/stay-abroad/stayabroadcoordinator.html).
