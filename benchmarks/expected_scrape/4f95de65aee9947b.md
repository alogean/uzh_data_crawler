# PhD Program in Finance

The Department of Finance at the University of Zurich offers a leading PhD program tailored to exceptional and committed students aspiring to excel in academia. Our rigorous curriculum and cutting-edge research environment are designed to educate the next generation of finance scholars and thought leaders.

Our program emphasizes both theoretical foundations and empirical applications, ensuring that graduates are well-equipped to tackle complex challenges in finance. Through a combination of advanced coursework, research seminars, and personal research projects, students develop the analytical skills and critical thinking abilities essential for success in academia and beyond.

As part of the Swiss Finance Institute (SFI) network, our department provides a unique opportunity for a rich academic experience. Students benefit from collaborations with top researchers, access to state-of-the-art resources, and exposure to a wide network of peers in partner institutions.

Embark on your doctoral journey with us and become part of a community dedicated to advancing financial knowledge and shaping the future of the field.
