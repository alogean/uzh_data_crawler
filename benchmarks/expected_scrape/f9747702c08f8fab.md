## PhD Program 2027 at the Department of Finance, UZH

You are talented, committed, and aspire to understand and shape the financial markets of tomorrow? Then apply and join our PhD program and community.

The PhD in Finance from the University of Zurich is a challenging, full-time program in Zürich. You will learn how to approach and answer complex financial and economic questions through thorough understanding and academic research, preparing you for a stimulating career in academia, industry, or government.

The program is fully funded, which means that you will be paid to do your research and gain valuable experience supporting our teaching activities.

## Welcome!

**We are excited to welcome our new PhD candidates, who joined our faculty in fall semester 2026/27!**

👏 We wish you a good start into your academic journey!

![](/dam/jcr:9d058516-7949-4814-9520-341aef794915/WS_UZH_DF_PhD_2026_27_20260922_140226.jpg)

From left to right: Prof. Andreas Fuster, Anass Rian, Sarah Wikus (Doctoral Program Coordinator), Haoting Dong, Daniele Cisilino, Alireza Tabib Pour (not in the picture)

Are you interested to pursue an academic career in finance? Our department offers a fully-funded SFI Swiss Finance Institute PhD program for students. Gain some insights of our newest PhD students from this program:

1. **Anass Rian:** "I completed my Bachelor's degree in Economics at Université Clermont Auvergne, spending one year abroad in Spain, and then my MRes in Finance at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My master's thesis looked at how data breaches affect firms' financing decisions. Alongside my studies, I worked as a Research Assistant for several finance professors and interned at the central bank of Morocco. These experiences inspired me to pursue a PhD. I chose the Department of Finance at the University of Zurich for its structured training, excellent advising, and supportive community of researchers."
2. **Haoting Dong:** "I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at Tsinghua University, where I developed a growing interest in academic research and decided to pursue a PhD in Finance. Since joining the Department of Finance at the University of Zurich, I have enjoyed the rigorous academic training as well as the supportive and approachable community of faculty and fellow PhD students.  I have also enjoyed settling into Zurich, especially walking through its streets lined with distinctive architecture."
3. **Daniele Cisilino:** "I completed my Master’s degree in Economics and Finance at the Einaudi Institute for Economics and Finance in Rome, where I also worked as a Research Assistant. During that time, I became particularly interested in how banks shape firms’ financing and investment decisions, which led me to pursue a PhD in Finance. I decided to join the Department of Finance at the University of Zurich and the Swiss Finance Institute because of their strong research environment, rigorous training, and the opportunity to interact with leading researchers in these areas. I am very much looking forward to the years ahead in Zurich, both academically and personally."
