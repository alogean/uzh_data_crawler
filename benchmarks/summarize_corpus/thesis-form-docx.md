IMPORTANT NOTES

The format of the form cannot be edited. If you need more rows in an area than available, please insert the course in another area and add the area which it belongs to in brackets.

In case a module does not correspond to the preformulated modules on this form, please cross out the listed module and note down the module you have attended instead by hand.

Minor program: Please insert all modules in the relevant table in this form. For minor programs offered by other faculties, the particular regulations of the respective faculty apply.

Basic principles applied for processing degrees:

The modules are credited toward the degree in ascending chronological order.

Surplus modules are not credited toward the degree.

If more modules than necessary were completed in a semester (usually in the last one) the modules with the better grades will be taken from this semester and included in the degree.

This form has to be submitted as an original to the Dean’s Office no later than the registration deadline for the selected degree conferral date.
